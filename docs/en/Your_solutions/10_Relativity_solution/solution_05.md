### The Calculation



$$m = \frac{m_0}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}}$$



* **$m$** = Relativistic mass
* **$m_0$** = Rest mass (**50 kg**)
* **$v$** = Velocity (**$0.999c$**)
* **$c$** = Speed of light



1. **Square the velocity fraction:**

$$\frac{v^2}{c^2} = (0.999)^2 = 0.998001$$


2. **Subtract from 1:**

$$1 - 0.998001 = 0.001999$$


3. **Take the square root:**

$$\sqrt{0.001999} \approx 0.04471$$


4. **Divide the rest mass by this factor:**

$$m = \frac{50}{0.04471}$$


$$m \approx 1118.3 \text{ kg}$$



**Answer:** The relativistic mass of the supermodel would be approximately **1,118.3 kg**.

### The Grain of Sand





$$m = \frac{m_0}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}}$$



1. **Square the velocity fraction:**

$$\frac{v^2}{c^2} = (0.999)^2 = 0.998001$$


2. **Subtract from 1:**

$$1 - 0.998001 = 0.001999$$


3. **Take the square root:**

$$\sqrt{0.001999} \approx 0.04471$$



*(This means the Lorentz factor $\gamma$ is $1 / 0.04471 \approx 22.366$, so the relativistic mass will be about 22.36 times its rest mass.)*
4. **Divide the rest mass by this factor:**

$$m = \frac{0.000001 \text{ kg}}{0.04471}$$


$$m \approx 0.00002236 \text{ kg}$$



**Answer:** The relativistic mass of the grain of sand would be approximately **$2.236 \times 10^{-5} \text{ kg}$** (or about **22.36 milligrams**).
